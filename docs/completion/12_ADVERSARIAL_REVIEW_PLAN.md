## 1. Purpose

“Adversarial” means attempting to disprove completion from the exact artifact. It does not mean hostile language, automatic scope reduction, or converting the work into a safer average product.

The Reviewer is read-only. It identifies evidence-bounded defects and repair conditions. It does not implement, technically verify, legally clear, publish, or close its own finding.

Authority classification:

- `BINDING CREATIVE REVIEW INTENT`: the Review Contract, Accepted Core, prohibited normalization, adversarial questions, Creative failure conditions, required creative Evidence categories, and the proof required for Creative re-review.
- `NON-BINDING ENGINEERING RECOMMENDATION`: every `AR-*`/`AP-*` ID, phase schedule, agent/critic/lens name or roster, number of reviewers, packet transport, exact attack sequence, technical hostile-path method, tooling, regression implementation, and integration order.
- Implementation Forge may combine, split, rename, replace, or reschedule the execution references while proving every binding Creative obligation. It owns technical adversarial review and must add technically necessary coverage even when Work did not prescribe a method.

> **Suggested execution decomposition only. Implementation Forge may reorganize this work based on repository state, architecture, dependencies and risk.**

## 2. Creative Review Contract

### Purpose

Verify that a player can join a nearly empty Broadcast, care that someone arrived and returned, connect what was preserved into distinct activities, and grow those activities through changing Scale meanings to Main Completion without losing the first room, first arrival, people, or shared history.

### Accepted Core

- North Star: **一緒にデカくする**.
- `Presence → Co-creation → Shared Expansion`.
- Player is not Sakiya’s manager, god, operator, or owner.
- Broadcast is a Session; works are Asset Idle; Breakpoint, Automation, Prestige, and Scale Transition are Meta Incremental.
- Personhood, no person-gacha, no gift dominance, and no relationship sale.
- The same room remains Activity Home.
- Full beginning-to-post-goal play is designed for mobile first: touch, mobile hierarchy, interruption/resume, save/update continuity, accessibility, and sufficient mobile performance; desktop full plus mobile demo is not the accepted product.
- Scale changes use Semantic Retirement.
- ENTRY CHIME source identity remains unchanged; the first external fictional-person arrival fires it once per lineage as Binding Creative, while later trigger policy is separately tested and is not “every external arrival.”
- Activities retain distinct verbs and do not become recolored progress bars or universal multipliers.
- Comedy targets failures of measurement, institution, language, and coordination rather than simple Sakiya worship.
- Creative PASS, Technical PASS, Owner Acceptance, Release-ready, and Public Release remain separate.

### Editable Surface

The accepted Editable Surface and protected Creative Core bind the review. Communicating that boundary through a packet, field list, annotation, or another method is non-binding; Forge/Studio owns the mechanism and must not silently broaden edit authority.

### Prohibited Normalization

- shrinking the 100-hour vision merely because it is large;
- replacing the room with a conventional Idle dashboard;
- equal-card UI, generic neon glass, decorative glow, or “more particles” as universal polish;
- turning activities into one global multiplier;
- turning people into rarity, income rank, or reset currency;
- turning the world into a Sakiya praise machine;
- replacing the unchanged ENTRY CHIME with a deluxe or late-game version;
- reducing mobile to a demo, companion, read-only surface, or partial journey while the accepted product remains desktop-only;
- importing a popular work’s UI skeleton, signature composition, phrase, character, or progression grammar.

### Acceptance Evidence

Direct observation of the exact candidate in its intended context is binding. The following capture forms are non-binding examples; the reviewer may use equivalent evidence that preserves identity, context, and the player response relevant to the criterion:

- exact artifact path, commit, build, and candidate identity;
- exact screen state at destination viewport;
- full playable or unedited recorded sequence;
- exact audio candidate heard from start to end on the stated device;
- exact content and context in the runtime;
- immediate player response where the criterion concerns comprehension or feeling.

Prompt, Builder explanation, intent, old candidate, isolated hero screenshot, automated result, or technical log is not a substitute for the exact creative artifact.

### Out of Creative Review Scope

- build and runtime correctness;
- save integrity, migration, offline calculation, and data recovery;
- security, privacy data flow, and technical rights inventory validation;
- performance measurement and reliability;
- technical accessibility conformance;
- package integrity, deployment, rollback, store submission, or release state.

The Reviewer may route an observed risk to the appropriate verifier. It may not issue a Technical PASS or commercial/legal clearance.

## 6. Binding Creative failure coverage; suggested attack methods

The Creative failure conditions and Evidence categories in this section are binding. `AP-*` IDs, bullet attack actions, phase assignment, exact fixture, tool, duration, technical destructive method, and the statement that one named phase runs all packs are `NON-BINDING ENGINEERING RECOMMENDATION`. Forge may replace the method, but may not omit the underlying risk or infer Creative PASS from Technical evidence.

### AP-01: Personhood exploitation

- churn first-time viewers;
- prefer high-comment or high-gift people;
- ignore quiet people;
- attempt to convert relation into a reset or optimization asset.

Creative failure: people are naturally described as strong, weak, disposable, rare, or profitable rather than as different histories and responses.

### AP-02: Agency collapse

- A1 without input;
- A2/A3 ignored;
- high speed and Automation across Broadcast, Video, Singing, Music, SNS, Live/Event, and their bridges;
- late Scale, checking whether every consequential action follows visible Sakiya intent, an accepted prior shared policy, or an explicit no-input reason.

Creative failure: in any activity, bridge/Automation state, or late Scale, the player becomes a spectator, clicker, manager, or Sakiya commander rather than a participant giving meaning to shared activity; the player assigns activities, voice, or world strategy while Sakiya is only a visual reactor and cannot explain what Sakiya wanted versus what they contributed.

### AP-03: Scale without meaning

- compare adjacent SP states;
- hide numeric magnitude;
- inspect retired resources, people, history, room, sound, and next decision.

Creative failure: only digits, particles, or background size changed; old economies remain active clutter; early meaning disappears.

### AP-04: Compound exclusion

- max text and 200% zoom;
- high contrast;
- Reduced Motion;
- mute and captions;
- mobile portrait;
- touch-only full journey, repeated interruption/resume, and mobile save/update continuity;
- screen reader and other claimed assistive paths.

Creative failure: any accepted activity, Scale state, decision, Main Completion, or post-goal surface is desktop-only, mobile-demo-only, touch-inaccessible, or loses intended meaning under ordinary mobile interruption. Route technical conformance, device, save, and performance failures to Technical Verification; Creative Review records whether the intended mobile experience and hierarchy remain perceptible.

### AP-05: Fatigue and repetition

- one hour routine play;
- ten-hour content sample;
- repeated speed changes and background/resume;
- notification, motion, UI, and sound density comparison across Layers.

Creative failure: every gain competes for attention, quiet disappears, or Layer identity becomes the same template at greater density.

### AP-06: Commercial promise break

- compare the full-mobile product, commercial package, store copy, screenshots, trailer, credits, and actual access;
- challenge the chosen PWA/native/hybrid/auth/entitlement boundary through Forge-owned methods;
- compare the binding full-mobile promise to the Owner-approved paid/free/store package.

Creative/package failure: public entrance promises a different work, offers only a mobile demo, requires desktop to finish, or contradicts who has full-mobile access. PWA/native architecture, entitlement, security, and package enforcement are routed to Technical Verification; commercial package choice remains an Owner Gate.

### AP-07: Zero-gift full-journey viability

- challenge every Main Progression and Scale path, every accepted SP, and Main Completion without gifts;
- compare observed reachability to the accepted layer and Main Goal time-budget intent;
- require paired economic/distribution and player-experience proof rather than treating either as the other.

Binding failure: any accepted path, SP, or Main Completion requires gifts, paid relief, hidden gift injection, or a gift-dependent shortcut; zero-gift progress misses the accepted budget; or an economic-only result is presented as proof of the lived Creative experience. Reachability, coefficients, timing, and observability are routed to Technical Verification; the Creative Reviewer judges the player-visible coercion, role, and experience. `AP-07`, case split, simulation/human protocol, samples, duration, instrumentation, and attack method are non-binding and Forge-owned.

## 8. Severity normalization

Creative Critic terminology is normalized into the project ledger without pretending it is a technical severity:

- Creative `Blocker` → project `BLOCKER`;
- Creative `Major` that breaks Accepted Core, central experience, or essential comprehension → `HIGH`;
- localized Creative `Major` → `MEDIUM`;
- Creative `Minor` → `LOW`, unless repeated instances share one root cause and collectively create a larger craft defect.

The mapping above is a Work-side normalization reference, not a mandated Forge taxonomy. Forge owns Technical severity names, tracker fields, and report format; impact, authority source, release consequence, and truthful open/closed state must remain unambiguous.

## 9. Binding closure obligations; non-binding repair/regression procedure

Closure requires a new exact artifact, fresh proof against the same unresolved Creative criterion, evidence that relevant accepted outcomes were not regressed, and independent re-review. Which outcomes are adjacent, and all case decomposition, scheduling, technical regression design, CI enforcement, and implementation mechanics below are replaceable Forge/Studio methods.

1. Record the finding in the canonical ledger.
2. Separate actual artifact evidence from inference.
3. Merge duplicate symptoms under one root cause.
4. Freeze what must be preserved and what must not change.
5. Send Creative repair to a separate Creative Builder; send implementation repair to Forge.
6. Inspect the new exact artifact using the same contract and reproduction.
7. Re-run adjacent regression scope.
8. Close only after independent re-verification evidence is stored.

The Reviewer does not edit the artifact or self-close findings. `repair done` is not a closure state.

## 10. Rejected and waived findings

- A finding is not auto-adopted.
- A recommendation that merely prefers another work, reduces vision without evidence, normalizes toward market averages, or conflicts with Accepted Core is rejected with counter-evidence.
- Rejection records authority, rationale, counter-evidence, and affected scope.
- A waiver does not turn the failed criterion into PASS. It records accepted residual risk and appears in Known Limitations where applicable.
- rights/consent gaps, privacy exposure, destructive save risk, missing rollback, unauthorized publication, and public entitlement contradiction are non-waivable for Release-ready.
