## 3. Global Activity Contract

The `ACT-*` identifiers are document IDs, not game terminology.

### ACT-01 — Every activity is a different game loop

Each activity defines:

1. a distinct player verb family;
2. a distinct short-term state or decision;
3. a distinct persistent output;
4. its own bottleneck and Acceleration point;
5. at least one typed Bridge to another activity;
6. at least one result that cannot be reproduced by a universal currency;
7. what remains manual after Automation;
8. what changes at Prestige and Scale Transition.

### ACT-02 — Player-visible resource budget

Each activity normally shows two to four primary resources at one time. Collections, histories, and analysis may exist outside the primary HUD.

If an activity needs more counters, it must demonstrate a distinct current decision for each. Displaying every historical resource simultaneously is not depth.

### ACT-03 — Session / Asset / Meta separation

- Session changes people, atmosphere, condition, and bounded events.
- Asset persists, works over time or offline, and carries provenance.
- Meta changes the rule, conversion, Automation, Prestige, or counted Scale.

An activity may touch all three, but one resource does not become all three at once.

### ACT-04 — Bridge grammar

Every Bridge specifies:

- typed source input;
- player transformation or routing decision;
- typed destination output;
- visible causal evidence;
- return path or downstream decision;
- positive-loop control;
- retirement behavior.

### ACT-05 — Automation preserves authorship and understanding

Automation may compress:

- repeated preparation;
- understood processing;
- batch operation;
- scheduled operation;
- background asset maintenance.

Automation does not silently remove:

- first use of a new activity;
- first use of a new Theme or work type;
- named-person important events;
- first Breakpoint, Prestige, Scale Peak, or major Event;
- the activity's identity-defining selection.

### ACT-06 — No universal reward

An activity is incomplete if its only contribution is a permanent multiplier to every other activity. A strong activity changes what the player can make, choose, route, observe, or synchronize.

### ACT-07 — Shared Agency requires Sakiya-side intent

Every consequential activity choice begins from a visible Sakiya-side intent, offer, boundary, or previously accepted policy. The player translates and contributes; they do not assign work to Sakiya or impersonate her. Sakiya may modify, postpone, or refuse a proposal. A result fails if the player can explain only “I assigned her to do X,” even when every economy number is valid.

| Activity | Sakiya-side independent intent | Player contribution | Forbidden authority shortcut |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streaming | offers a Theme, purpose, experiment, or boundary for the Broadcast | prepares, notices, reacts, comments, preserves, and connects | scheduling her as an obedient production unit |
| Video | proposes what she wants to show, remember, or communicate | selects provenance, shape, destination, and return tradeoff among accepted options | forcing publication or rewriting the event's meaning |
| Vocal | offers an expression/mastery focus and acceptable take boundary | supports practice, observes change, chooses what to preserve or route | forcing a take or treating condition as consumable labor |
| Music | brings a fragment, motive, or meaning she wants to develop | connects arrangement, context, audience, and version choices | overwriting her voice because a higher-output option exists |
| SNS | provides intended message and a no-send/edit boundary | chooses timing/context among offered options and interprets response | impersonating Sakiya or posting outside her accepted intent |
| Live Event | sets purpose and acceptable commitments | combines routes, preparation, participation, and aftermath choices | committing Sakiya to an event or promise without confirmation |

### ACT-08 — Action phases remain activity-specific

| Activity | Frame / prepare | Perform / transform | Interpret / return |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streaming | choose Theme, accepted purpose, Material, and participation stance | observe, react/comment or remain present during LIVE | preserve a moment, review people/events, connect the next step |
| Video | select provenance-bearing Material and intended context | shape, complete, and publish the work | read long-tail response and route a contextual return to Broadcast/another activity |
| Vocal | accept a focus, condition, and expression boundary | practice, listen, improve, accelerate, or master | preserve a take/trait and decide whether it should feed Music or a Session |
| Music | select fragments, meaning, and version purpose | arrange, combine, finish, and release | curate the work's long tail and connect its context without flattening it into Reach |
| SNS | choose an eligible intended message, context, and timing window | post, react, and observe spread | let short-cycle state decay; archive or bridge only the meaningful result |
| Live Event | agree purpose, commitments, inputs, and preparation | gather, participate, respond, and intensify in a bounded Session | close commitments, read aftermath, and preserve only typed outputs/history |

### ACT-09 — Resource lifetime ledger

Working names remain replaceable; the lifetime and personhood constraints are Binding Creative.

| Activity | Source | Active lifetime and use | Retirement / persistence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Streaming | real Session events, accepted Theme, participant presence | LIVE/HYPE and attention are Session-bounded; choices create relationship events and typed Materials | Session counters expire; people/history persist; selected Highlight/Archive becomes a provenance-bearing Asset |
| Video | selected Highlight/Material/Archive | production state lasts until completed/abandoned; published Video works as Asset Idle; response aggregates explain route performance | unused Material is archived or deliberately discarded; published work/history persists until Semantic Retirement freezes its economy |
| Vocal | accepted focus, current condition, prior mastery | condition is Session-bounded; practice changes persistent capability; takes/traits carry provenance | condition expires; mastery and accepted takes persist/transform; no person state is spent |
| Music | fragments, takes, Theme/meaning, accepted versions | versioned work persists through production; released work has a long-tail Asset life | fragments are consumed only into traceable versions or archived; the work/history persists while old live production may retire |
| SNS | Sakiya-approved message/context and typed source Asset | spread/reaction window is short-lived; Post persists; Reach/follower-like values are aggregate context, never spendable people | short-cycle spread expires; meaningful Post/context enters history or a typed Bridge; no permanent global multiplier remains |
| Live Event | accepted purpose, commitments, activity inputs, cohort context | attendance/excitement are bounded Session state; preparation and response remain interactive | Session totals expire; record/aftermath/relationship events persist as typed outputs/history, not a universal currency |

### ACT-10 — Mobile completeness without activity collapse

Every activity's three action phases, Sakiya-side intent, typed resources, Automation choice, Bridge provenance, failure/recovery, and Scale transformation remain operable by touch in phone portrait. A focused sheet or sequential comparison may replace simultaneous desktop panels, but it cannot delete an option, obscure opportunity cost, automate an ununderstood decision, or make one activity the mobile shortcut.

Typing, precision dragging, multi-finger gestures, rapid/repeated tapping, hover, and permanent foreground time are never required for Main Progression. Background/resume returns to the exact phase with a concise causal summary; it never completes a consequential Sakiya/player choice silently or duplicates output. No activity may be cut, simplified into a companion action, or made desktop-only to meet mobile constraints.

Video does not merely increase first-arrival volume. It changes at least one of:

- what interests an arriving person;
- what topic they recognize;
- what reaction is likely;

- diminishing long-tail response;
- recovering arrival opportunity;
- provenance-specific reach;
- no relationship or mastery purchase with views;
- no universal activity multiplier from subscribers.

### Activity completion acceptance

- A video-sourced arrival is distinguishable in the next stream's cause log.
- Removing the video Bridge removes a unique contextual choice, not only a percentage bonus.
- The video-focused strategy does not Pareto-dominate all legal P0 strategies.
- Video remains different from music production and SNS amplification.

## 12. Bridge acceptance contract

A Bridge passes only when:

1. its input remains attributable to its source;
2. the player makes a transformation or routing decision;
3. the destination receives a typed result;
4. the result changes a choice, build, circulation, or bottleneck;
5. its positive loop has continuation control;
6. its old form can aggregate or retire later;
7. removing it removes a unique decision, not only a percentage bonus.

`all production +10%` fails regardless of balance.

## 14. Anti-universal-resource review

### Forbidden outcomes

- 認知 buys output in all six activities.
- ファン becomes a spendable population, workforce, or Prestige Material.
- 熱量 can be banked into permanent global progress.
- video views, music plays, Reach, and Followers immediately merge into one power value.
- SNS grants a permanent multiplier to every activity.
- live event returns the same reward to every activity.
- a Prestige currency grants only the same multiplier everywhere.
- relationship depth is paid or consumed.
- a Scale Unit becomes a spendable resource.

### Pass conditions

- Each activity displays no more primary resources than it needs for current decisions.
- Horizontal resources connect and explain but do not replace activity-local choice.
- Every Bridge retains source context.
- Removing an activity removes a unique verb and circulation rather than only output magnitude.
- Obsolete automated resources aggregate or leave the primary HUD.
- Scale Peak stops old Unit live generation while keeping history and basis.

- Vocal and music receive different descriptions.
- SNS is not described as an all-production boost.
- Live event is not described as a reward screen.

- Automation preserves the activity's identity-defining choice.

- The tester distinguishes frame/preparation, performance/transformation, and interpretation/return without describing every activity as the same producer button.
- The tester identifies what expires, what persists, and what becomes history without treating people as inventory.
- On the full mobile artifact, every activity completes its full action/Bridge/recovery loop by touch with no desktop-only data or strategy, and interruption resumes the correct phase without duplicate output.
