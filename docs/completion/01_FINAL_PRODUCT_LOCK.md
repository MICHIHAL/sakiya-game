## 1. Locked product proposition

Working title: 「八乙女さきや 活動者育成インクリメンタル」  
North Star: 「一緒にデカくする。」

The player joins Sakiya's activity as a participant, not as a manager, god, owner, or optimizer standing outside it. A nearly empty stream becomes a remembered place; what happens there becomes a work; that work brings different people back; distinct activities create new bridges; the activity expands until the current unit of measurement stops explaining it. At every scale, the first room, named fictional participants, and the exact first-arrival sound remain meaningful.

Creative promise:

> A creator-incremental game in which the player can emotionally participate from the first minute, build genuine continuity with fictional people and works, and still enjoy accelerating systems and absurd scale without reducing anyone to a resource.

Participation arc:

1. Presence — I was there when someone arrived.
2. Co-creation — what we chose to preserve became something.
3. Shared Expansion — what we made changes who can arrive and what the activity can become.

## 3. Player role lock

### ACCEPTED CORE

- The player translates Sakiya-side intentions and experiments into choices while participating emotionally.
- The player may choose plans, reactions, preservation, connections, and what to release at Prestige or Scale.
- The player does not author both sides of a fake conversation or command Sakiya's identity.
- A participation profile is optional and has no rarity, performance, productivity, or payment power.
- Player silence may be a valid form of participation; it cannot be punished into compulsory input.

### Role failure

Reject a build if players primarily describe themselves as Sakiya's manager, a god, a factory operator, or an external dashboard optimizer.

## 5. Core loop and domain lock

The complete product uses three domains:

| Domain | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Session | bounded shared event with attention now | Broadcast LIVE, live performance, special event |
| Asset Idle | a work continues to reach or return value | video, music, archive, clip |
| Meta Incremental | rules, bridges, acceleration, and measurement change | synergy, Breakpoint, automation, Prestige, Scale Peak |

The first required loop is:

`Before → LIVE → After → preserve a highlight/material → make and publish a Video → Video reaches a different interest → next Broadcast changes → first Cross-Activity Synergy`

Before, LIVE, and After remain different experiences. Only understood LIVE time may compress; firsts, important person events, and consequential After decisions remain visible and reviewable.

## 6. Social and personhood lock

- Participants are fictional by default and have stable identities across absence and revisit.
- Relationship depth is persistent and masked; it is a state, not a spendable currency.
- Quiet presence, reaction, comment, absence, return, and CRITICAL may all be meaningful.
- A CRITICAL event must have perceptible precursors and an After explanation; it is not a jackpot.
- A regular cohort is a history-bearing group, not a collection of production modifiers.
- Gifts are optional events. They cannot buy relationship, unlock progression, create a best listener, or form the dominant route.
- All Main Progression and Scale paths must pass a zero-gift paired test within the same time budget.
- Prestige, Scale, Strong New Game, and completion do not reroll or consume people.
- Permanent departure is not accepted. Reversible distance/absence is the Work recommendation pending Owner decision.

## 7. Activity lock

| Activity | Primary form | Core verbs | Distinct value |
|---|---|---|---|
| streaming | social Session | participate, meet, remain, grow | named continuity and the origin of shared material |
| video | production + Asset Idle | make, publish, reach, grow | turns a shared moment into asynchronous discovery |
| singing | mastery action | practice, shorten, master | repetition becomes capability and expressive choice |
| music | production + collection + Asset Idle | compose, complete, be heard | slow, durable work connecting several systems |
| SNS | short-cycle amplifier | send, respond, spread | immediate, selective context propagation with decay |
| live event | cross-system Session | gather, intensify, erupt | finite orchestration peak with activity-specific aftermath |

Archive and clips are assets/automation candidates, not mandatory seventh activities. Community is a relationship and Scale foundation, not an extractive workforce.

Every activity needs its own action grammar, 2–4 primary resources or states, acceleration path, return path, limiter, failure states, and test. A universal resource, universal progress bar, or global percentage button cannot substitute for an activity.

## 8. Economy and progression lock

Main Progression is not one currency. It is evidence that the current law of growth can change:

`relationship continuity AND current-activity proof AND provenance-bearing bridge AND a newly created decision`

The active bottleneck moves to the slowest unmet proof. The exact formal bottleneck and coefficients remain UNKNOWN until P0-SIM.

Resource rules:

- Every visible resource or state declares source, current decision, transformation/retirement, HUD lifetime, and historical remainder.
- Bridges preserve activity, event, theme, and interest provenance.
- Positive loops require at least one continuing control: diminishing return, cap, another resource, recovering pool, temporary state, or Prestige boundary.
- No-wait: when an Asset Idle process runs, another meaningful active choice is available.
- No-dead-resource: a resource that stops affecting choices is transformed, aggregated, historical, hidden, or semantically retired rather than granted a fake sink.
- Offline progression belongs to Asset Idle and understood automation; short-session HYPE/condition does not bank.
- Important named-person events cannot finish invisibly while offline; they return through history or a replayable summary.

## 10. Prestige and Scale lock

Prestige releases a current economy or growth law and creates a new decision. It may reset current reach, current concurrency, temporary ranks, equipment/economy, or layer-local production. It does not delete identity, relationship history, representative works, firsts, the room, or the proof that made the prior layer possible.

Scale Peak changes the unit through which growth is understood. U0–U10 are the working ontology:

`individual participation → stream viewers → community → content network → activity ecosystem → cultural reach → social infrastructure → planetary attention → civilization observation → interstellar observation → cosmic resonance`

On retirement, old-unit live production stops. Final value, history, people, works, and basis remain inspectable. A smaller counter must not keep generating behind the new dashboard.

## 11. World, room, visual, and audio lock

- Final navigation is rooted in Activity Home. It opens contextual Activity workspaces plus People/History, Works/Archive, Progress/World, and Settings/Help; important events deep-link to their cause and return focus to the invoking control.
- Activity Home always exposes: current intent, what changed, the next meaningful action, current people/works, and a reversible way to inspect detail. It is not six equal dashboard cards.
- Broadcast's final screen keeps Sakiya/room and named events primary, LIVE state secondary, and acceleration/resource detail tertiary. Before and After use focused choice sheets rather than copying the LIVE HUD.
- Save/import/update/offline/audio-unlock, empty/locked/cooldown/failure/recovery, accessibility, Prestige/Scale receipt, ending, Continue, and Strong New Game are first-class states, not generic modals added later.
- The room is the permanent Activity Home, not a disposable tutorial scene.
- Scale appears by changing what intrudes into the room, not by replacing it with an abstract space spreadsheet.
- Early visuals foreground people and moments; later visuals add system, geography, institution, planetary, and cosmic evidence while maintaining legibility.
- The Work visual proposition is intimate creator-room realism interrupted by increasingly impossible but causally specific evidence. Matte room materials and warm human focus remain the base; system/world intrusions use cooler, sharper, domain-specific materials rather than generic neon/glass/cosmic glow.
- Sakiya's canonical visual reference requires Owner acceptance before final character asset production.
- Placeholder geometry, text-only cosmic dashboards, and auto-generated final assets cannot count as finish evidence.
- Whenever the `ENTRY CHIME` is used, it resolves to the exact same source asset.
- No pitch shift, remix, layer, deluxe variant, or fake finale reproduction is allowed.
- Trigger policy is separate from asset identity. The first external fictional-person arrival fires the exact ENTRY CHIME once per save lineage; this first-arrival trigger is Binding Creative. A later deliberately foregrounded named fictional-person arrival is a Work recommendation and remains TEST-DEPENDENT / Owner-conditional. Anonymous/bulk arrivals, offline generation, ordinary Scale output, and fake arrivals do not trigger it.
- A Main Completion arrival may trigger it only if the proposed completion sequence is Owner-accepted.
- The invariant sound receives a simultaneous visual/caption cue and survives muted/audio-disabled play.
- Music may widen in orchestration and space with Scale, but must leave quiet room moments and priority for person events.

## 13. Mobile-first platform, accessibility, save, and release lock

### OWNER ACCEPTED CREATIVE REQUIREMENT — SRC-OWNER-MOBILE-01

- the complete experience—from first launch and first Broadcast through every activity, BP/SP/Prestige, Main Completion, Continue, and Strong New Game—is playable on a supported mobile device;
- mobile portrait and touch are primary design conditions, not a reduced companion/demo surface;
- mobile landscape, interruption/background-resume, safe areas, software keyboard, thermal/memory limits, offline return, and short-session re-entry must preserve state, causality, and emotional continuity;
- no rule, strategy, history, comparison, creative choice, accessibility control, or completion path is desktop-only;
- desktop may also receive the full product, but desktop support cannot be used to waive mobile completeness.

Implementation architecture remains Forge-owned. Forge must compare repository-grounded mobile delivery and entitlement alternatives—such as an installable entitled PWA, native-store package, or shared core/wrapper—together with offline/update/save, privacy/security, store, support, performance, and rollback tradeoffs. SAKIYA selects the commercial package through `ODG-09`; the mobile full-experience requirement itself is already accepted through `ODG-11`. A public demo may exist only as a supplementary surface and cannot satisfy mobile-full acceptance.

The Work business recommendation remains buy-to-play with no microtransactions, paid relationship path, or gift purchase; price/store/entitlement mechanics are not selected here. Cross-device sync is not implied: local-first complete mobile play may pass without an account if the chosen distribution allows it.

Minimum interaction target is 44 × 44 CSS px for primary controls. Touch, keyboard/pointer where supported, reduced motion, contrast, text scaling, audio-independent cues, focus/reading order, and portrait layouts are release criteria. Save needs a new namespace/schema, local-first autosave, export/import, corruption recovery, explicit update migration, and mobile lifecycle recovery. Cloud, account, telemetry, and real-person data are excluded by default unless the selected entitlement/sync route explicitly expands and clears that scope.

Public Release requires separate Owner authorization after current build, evidence, rights, privacy, commercial, store, and rollback gates pass. Branch creation or an owner-only preview is not Public Release.
