## 2. Business and platform boundary

The Work recommendation is a buy-to-play complete base game with no gameplay microtransactions. It protects gifts and relationships from purchase pressure and allows the 100-hour hypothesis to be balanced as one work.

Forbidden monetization:

- selling relationship progress, listener performance, or person rarity;
- paid gift probability, gift multipliers, or gifts as the optimal progression route;
- selling relief from intentionally created waiting;
- randomized paid person acquisition;
- power attached to identity or participation-profile cosmetics;
- subscriptions that hold a local save or relationship history hostage.

Allowed candidates, each still requiring an Owner decision and rights/platform review:

- one-time purchase of the complete base game;
- a later substantial authored expansion that adds real activity, world, or post-goal content;
- a separately sold soundtrack or non-gameplay cosmetic package with cleared rights;
- a public demo with an explicit content and save boundary.

The full-product mobile premise is Owner accepted in `09_ACCESSIBILITY_PLATFORM_RELEASE_UX.md` and `ODG-11`. A paid desktop product plus public mobile demo is no longer an eligible complete-product route. Forge must propose and prove a full-mobile delivery/entitlement architecture; SAKIYA later accepts the commercial/store/public promise through `ODG-09`. A public demo is optional and supplementary.

## 4. Player identity and profile default

The default participation profile uses:

- a player-entered display label stored locally;
- a **preset-only profile icon** selected from cleared, fictional production assets;
- local play history and participation choices.

Custom icon/photo upload, camera capture, external avatar import, public profile publishing, voice capture, and free-text public comments are **out of base scope**. Adding any of them requires a new moderation, copyright, child-safety, privacy, storage, deletion, abuse, and security design. Profile label validation must prevent it from becoming an unreviewed public/store/telemetry surface.

The economy never requires a real person's data. Fictional listeners cannot be presented as real community members, and no fictional listener is tied to a real account or performance ranking.

## 5. Consent, scope, and withdrawal

Consent is item-specific and surface-specific; community participation, credits, marketing, in-game use, and model/tool input are never bundled.

Binding consent evidence must establish the following scope, authority, withdrawal, limitation, and actual-surface meanings. Record count, form, field names, storage, approval/signature workflow, and legal-review method are selected by the qualified reviewer:

- person/contributor identifier and authority to grant, with private contact data kept outside release manifests;
- exact asset, excerpt, name, likeness, comment, or performance;
- purposes and surfaces: game, demo, store, trailer, screenshot, social, press, archive, and credits;
- public/private status, platforms, territories, languages, term, compensation, credit, edit/translation/derivative permission, and distributor/subprocessor scope;
- minor status and guardian/qualified-review requirements; minors are excluded by default;
- withdrawal method, effective date, replacement/takedown response owner, limits for already-downloaded copies or lawful archives, and dispute/support path.

Withdrawal must not delete unrelated player progress. A withdrawn listener identity is removed, replaced, or irreversibly remapped to a fictional identity while preserving only non-identifying progression. New builds, store assets, hosted pages, and promotional files stop using the item according to the signed scope and approved response plan. Consent terms/evidence must not promise deletion from already-distributed offline copies when that cannot technically or legally be guaranteed; that limitation is disclosed before consent.

Consent evidence is access-controlled. Shipping/public evidence exposes only the minimum clearance reference/status needed for audit and never private contact data; the reference mechanism and record topology are reviewer/Forge-owned.

## 7. Entitlement and save-transfer contract

A save is data, never proof of purchase. Import, cloud restore, demo progression, profile name, and local flags cannot mint entitlement.

| Delivery class | Entitlement authority | Offline behavior to design | Save continuity | Owner/commercial decisions still required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **entitled installable web/PWA full product** | Owner-selected seller/account/token authority | entitlement cache/lease, expiry, clock anomaly, outage grace, storage eviction, recovery and revocation without trapping local history | local-first complete save; optional account sync/export requires conflict, deletion, recovery, and orphan rules | seller/controller/processors, authentication/recovery, security, purchase model, offline period, regions, support |
| **native mobile-store package** | each selected mobile store | purchase restore, refund/revocation, store outage, offline launch and device replacement | local full save; platform cloud or manual export only after explicit design | store(s), purchase coverage, family/platform sharing, restore, cloud/export, refund and support UX |
| **shared core/wrapper or Forge alternative** | selected store/seller appropriate to each package | equivalent offline entitlement and recoverable lifecycle on every promised surface | one Creative/system rule set; transfer/sync is optional and separately evidenced | package relation, purchase boundaries, DRM/no-DRM, update/migration, support, rollback |

No route may satisfy this table with a desktop-only full product and mobile demo. Exact implementation and the final route are determined after Forge's repository/architecture analysis; a save remains data and cannot mint entitlement.

For any approved demo-to-full transfer:

- the demo exports a documented, versioned data subset and never full locked content;
- the full product validates schema, type, range, origin/version, size, and integrity and strips executable/script content;
- import previews exactly what is retained, reset, or transformed and keeps the pre-import save recoverable;
- a full save does not import back into the public demo if doing so exposes paid content or private history;
- transfer success/failure is durable and supportable; it never changes the purchase/refund state.
