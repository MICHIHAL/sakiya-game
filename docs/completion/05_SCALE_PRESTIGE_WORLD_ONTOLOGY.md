## 3. Event separation contract

| Event | What changes | Semantic Retirement | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| First Event | an emotional first such as arrival, exit, or revisit | none | outside the 34 Major Events |
| Breakpoint | production, judgment, conversion, or the play grammar | none | 24 BP |
| Automation unlock | compresses already-understood work | none | not automatically a BP |
| Prestige Peak | releases current economy and updates the growth law | normally none; the same Unit may restart under a new law | exact count UNKNOWN |
| Scale Peak | replaces the current economic Unit | mandatory | 10 SP |

Macro Layer changes do not themselves perform Semantic Retirement. A Layer is a chapter-level change in world, UX, and abstraction. A Scale Peak is the exact event at which the economic unit changes.

If a Breakpoint unlocks Prestige, they still receive separate event IDs and player-facing receipts. If Prestige and Scale occur near each other, the player must separately understand:

- what current economy was released;
- what history remained;
- which Unit ended permanently;
- which new Unit became causal.

## 4. Common Scale Trigger contract

SP2–SP10 normally require all seven conditions. SP1 has a documented exception because its Trigger is unresolved.

1. **Comprehension** — the player has experienced the current Unit’s production, sink, bottleneck, and main decision.
2. **Causal saturation** — the current Unit no longer explains why two apparently equal states produce different outcomes.
3. **Emergent Unit** — the next Unit has a distinct production source, sink, bottleneck, and player decision.
4. **Cross-system proof** — more than one independent activity or route contributes; one universal route is insufficient.
5. **Continuity package** — people, representative events, final values, works, and room evidence can be preserved.
6. **Visible transition** — the player can explain the old Unit, the new Unit, and why the change happened.
7. **Retirement verification** — after the SP, the old persistent economy is frozen and only the new Unit progresses in real time.

A raw number threshold alone cannot trigger a Scale Peak. Forge proposes numeric thresholds only after the creative causal contract is testable.

## 8.1 Adjacent Major Event separation contract

Two Major Events remain separate even when their timings are adjacent. In particular, a BP changes a production rule or decision inside the current Unit; an SP retires that Unit and establishes a new economic meaning.

- each event has a distinct ID and trigger predicate;
- each has its own Moment, player-visible choice/result, receipt, and history/replay entry;
- between the BP and SP, the player makes at least one new judgment enabled by the BP, and the later SP has separate evidence that the old Unit can no longer explain that judgment;
- one animation, threshold crossing, or state write cannot silently count for both;
- save/reload and interruption reconstruction preserve both causal records;
- the player can recall what the BP changed and why the SP later made the old Unit insufficient.

This contract applies to every adjacency, including BP18/SP6 and any eventual BP/SP arrangement selected after testing.

## 9. World ontology contract

1. **A person is not a Scale Unit.** Person existence and history survive every Unit transition.
2. **A Unit is a current economic representation.** Retiring a Unit does not delete the underlying people, works, or activities.
3. **History is separate from live accumulation.** A final value may stop while first arrival, revisit, regularization, representative works, and major events remain inspectable.
4. **Activity grammar persists.** Broadcast, Video, Singing, Music, SNS, and Live do not become recolored generators of one cosmic currency.
5. **The world has agency.** practical work, opposition, adaptation, bandwagoning, and resignation prevent praise-only worldbuilding.
6. **Sakiya remains human.** surprise, embarrassment, confusion, and joy remain available without turning Sakiya into a deity or mascot.
7. **Sakiya retains independent intent.** A consequential late-Scale route derives from a visible Sakiya intent, an accepted prior policy, or an explicit reason no player input is requested. Sakiya may modify, postpone, or refuse; the player contributes translation and connection, not managerial assignment.
8. **Mobile keeps semantic Scale complete.** Every BP/SP Moment, old-Unit insufficiency, decision, Retirement receipt, history basis, U10 configuration, and post-goal route is readable and operable on the full mobile artifact; desktop is not the only surface that explains or controls Scale.

### Shared Agency at late Scale

Planetary or cosmic scope does not transfer ownership of Sakiya to the player. Every representative late-Scale decision must let a tester state “Sakiya wanted/offered X; I contributed Y.” A route that is valid only because the player commands Sakiya to maximize an abstract total fails even if the economy is balanced.

### Semantic Retirement receipt

Every SP shows:

- Unit that ended;
- final value;
- first and representative events;
- named people and cohorts;
- representative works;
- basis of the new Unit;
- explicit notice that the old persistent economy no longer grows;
- the first new decision made possible by the new Unit.

A human-equivalent or legacy view may remain as a read-only derived display. It cannot be an old economy still accumulating off-screen.

| Transition | Missing explanatory power | Institutional / accounting failure | Language failure | Dry-comedy principle |

| U3→U4 | content totals cannot represent mutual activity production | single metrics double-count loops | “content” conflates Session, Asset, Mastery, and Amplifier | the more exact the sum, the more often it counts the same result |

### U10 stable-cycle semantic contract — WORK RECOMMENDED HYPOTHESIS

U10 is not “an even larger audience.” Its working economy is a bounded choice about how provenance-bearing meaning propagates when source/receiver and local/global categories no longer explain the result.

| Element | Player-visible contract |
|---|---|
| Sources | accepted works, histories, relationships, activity-specific signals, and observation-zone routes preserved from U0–U9; a person is never an amount to spend |
| Use / bounded sink | commit a selected provenance set and synchronization capacity to one propagation configuration; incompatible routes remain local, delayed, or archived rather than everything receiving one universal bonus |

| Decision | choose which history/work/relationship basis should resonate through which connection, and which route should remain local; Sakiya may confirm, modify, postpone, or refuse |
| Result | a traceable resonance pattern changes later reception/return paths while every contributing basis remains inspectable |

1. at least two distinct valid propagation configurations with different opportunity costs;
2. a bounded or controlled positive loop rather than unconditional self-multiplication;
3. no retired U0–U9 Unit resumes live generation;
4. no single activity or scalar resource is sufficient for every configuration;
5. the player can explain the chosen sources, why another route remained local, and what later result changed;
6. Sakiya-side intent and the player's contribution are separately visible;
7. the room, people, works, DAY 1 history, and every Retirement basis remain directly accessible.
