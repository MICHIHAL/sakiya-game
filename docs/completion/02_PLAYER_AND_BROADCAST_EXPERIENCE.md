## 1. Experience promise

The player joins a stream that was almost empty. Someone arrives. The same name returns. What the player and Sakiya preserve from that stream becomes a work, and that work brings someone different to the next stream. The shared activity grows through several economies until the world can no longer measure it, without losing the memory of the first room and first arrival.

The promise, role, player-visible experience and failure/acceptance intent are Binding Creative / Binding Test Intent. Probe implementation, exact scripts/cases, participant method, instrumentation, and technical realization are Forge-owned.

The complete arc is mobile-first: a player using touch on a supported phone can begin, participate, preserve, build every activity, understand every BP/SP, complete U10, enter Continue, and begin Strong New Game. App switching, calls, screen lock, orientation changes, and short sessions preserve emotional and causal orientation; desktop never contains the only viable action or explanation.

## 2. Accepted player role

The player is not Sakiya’s manager, god, operator, or owner.

The player translates Sakiya’s activity intentions and experiments into game choices while emotionally participating from the start.

| Moment | Consistent role | Role-breaking behavior |
|---|---|---|
| participation profile | appears in the activity without performance | stats, rarity, paid power |
| Before | chooses what to do together from Sakiya-side possibilities | authors both sides of a fake conversation |
| LIVE | observes, notices, and optionally reacts | commands Sakiya’s personality or speech |
| After | chooses what should remain and connect | filters people only by income |
| cross-activity | gives meaning and destination to results | external administrator optimizing identical factories |
| Prestige | chooses what current economy to release and what meaning remains | erases people or history |
| Scale | sends activities toward a new unit from the same room | abandons room and Sakiya for an abstract dashboard |
| post-goal | continues as the participant who remembers | converts relationships into reset currency |

Creative PASS requires players to say “I was doing this with Sakiya,” not “I was controlling Sakiya.”

## 5. Broadcast loop contract

### Before

Purpose: decide what to do together.

Verbs: choose, bring, combine, set intent.

Choices come from current state and Sakiya-side possibilities. The player does not write both Sakiya’s desire and the profile’s response.

### LIVE

Purpose: be present for the event.

Verbs: watch, notice, react, comment, relate.

The game does not require per-second clicking. Important arrivals, exits, revisits, CRITICAL precursors, and firsts can pause and focus.

### After

Purpose: decide what remains and what it connects to.

Verbs: choose, preserve, reflect, connect.

Outputs may include a highlighted moment, archive, material, relationship record, or next intent.

## 9. Personhood and continuity

- Quiet viewers and active commenters can both create meaningful events.
- Difference appears in relationship history and response, not combat-style strength.
- Gifts cannot substitute for stay, revisit, or shared context.
- Initial cohort contains several people over time, not one immortal best unit.
- Prestige does not reroll people.
- Real listener names and comments are excluded by default.

P0 PASS:

- testers do not call the quiet person weak or disposable;
- a revisit is described as “the same person returned,” not “a better roll”;
- CRITICAL is explained as accumulated fit;
- another Broadcast is wanted without a gift.

## 11. CRITICAL probe

Preconditions:

- at least two prior Broadcasts;
- at least three meaningful contacts;
- current theme connects to prior response;
- exact relationship value hidden.

Sequence:

1. choose a theme connected to history;
2. show indirect precursors through stay, reaction, comment flow, and atmosphere;
3. allow the candidate A1/A2/A3 behavior;
4. trigger CRITICAL only when accumulated state and current context fit;
5. After explains the same causes that were perceptible beforehand.

PASS:

- player says “it had been building,” not “I rolled it”;
- one optional input did not purchase the event;
- After explanation matches visible precursors.
