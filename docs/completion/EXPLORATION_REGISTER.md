## 3. P0 Pareto Test Intent

This section binds comparison outcomes and disclosure, not the executable test structure.

No aggregate score may conceal tradeoffs. Compare at least:

- BP1–3 and first-synergy timing;
- meaningful return/revisit/cohort formation;
- player wait and repeated-action burden;
- person-churn advantage;
- Video or other route dominance;
- source-causal comprehension;
- LIVE role/fatigue/recall from P0-FEEL;
- positive-loop convergence;
- implementation complexity as a disclosed constraint, not the creative winner.

A candidate is dominated if another candidate is no worse on every accepted outcome and meaningfully better on at least one, with uncertainty disclosed. Non-dominated candidates proceed to creative judgment; simulation does not replace Sakiya acceptance.

## 4. Exploration failure rules

Close or redesign an exploration if:

- it changes an Accepted Core invariant merely to simplify implementation;
- the test cannot distinguish its candidates;
- it has no player-visible outcome;
- it promotes hidden data mining, real-person ingestion, or paid relationship;
- it treats numerical speed as the only value;
- it creates a universal resource or permanent optimal activity;
- it can fire SP1 during P0;
- it relies on placeholder quality to judge final feel;
- the evidence cannot be reproduced from source commit, configuration, build, applicable randomness control, and documented technical protocol.

## 6. Experiment record template

These are BINDING TEST INTENT evidence categories. Forge may adapt the artifact schema and tooling, but returned evidence must make each applicable category auditable:

- Exploration ID and decision IDs affected;
- source commit, build, configuration, applicable randomness control/seed, device class, and date;
- hypotheses and forbidden outcomes before the run;
- raw artifacts and summarized distributions;
- human protocol, participant count/limits, and consent where applicable;
- observed tradeoffs and uncertainty;
- dominated/non-dominated result;
- creative interpretation separated from technical interpretation;
- new risks/findings;
- recommendation, authority, and next gate;
- regression scope if adopted.

An experiment with missing reproducible artifacts remains INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE, even when its summary supports the preferred candidate. Forge determines the technical artifact format, retention method, and regression implementation.
