## 2. Tone and authority contract

The joke target is not “Sakiya is a god.” It is the failure of measurements, institutions, procedures, scientific language, and ordinary social systems to process the scale of the activity.

Required:

- Sakiya remains a person who enjoys, tries, reacts, gets confused, is pleased, and may resist the situation.
- Fictional listeners and communities have their own motives, continuity, boundaries, disagreements, and responses.
- The world may investigate, regulate, adapt, exploit, object, normalize, or misunderstand.
- Praise is one reaction among many, not the world engine.
- Each layer changes the causal problem instead of paraphrasing “the number is huge.”
- Official Sakiya wording becomes Canon only after Owner adoption.
- AI-added world details, histories, relationships, and names remain proposals until adopted.

Forbidden:

- universal admiration or self-deification as the only escalation;
- real listeners copied into the default cast;
- generic motivational filler;
- repeated “the system cannot count it” wording without a new cause or consequence;
- AI-written Sakiya lines silently presented as authentic;
- content volume used as proof of variety, personhood, or tone quality.

## 3. Counting ontology — do not merge these units

The prior materials use “Anchor,” “content unit,” and “exposure” at incompatible levels. Production evidence must preserve three non-mergeable identities and their relationships. The AEP/USP/NE names, IDs, and three-ledger model below are a **replaceable reference schema**; Forge may reorganize storage and identifiers if every semantic distinction, join, count, provenance fact, and acceptance query remains demonstrable.

| Unit | Exact definition | Counted when | Not equivalent to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Anchor Event Package (AEP)** | one complete causal event specification: trigger, prior state, state change, involved entities, ordered reaction surfaces, consequence, archive fact, and next possibility | one stable `AEP_ID` passes package review | one line, one reaction surface, or one player exposure |
| **Unique Surface Payload (USP)** | one uniquely authored deliverable for one reaction surface and context, with stable `USP_ID`, status, language, and source; e.g. a fictional-listener line, system notice, room-object response, or archive sentence | one distinct production payload exists in the registry | an AEP, raw display, or novelty event |
| **Novel Exposure (NE)** | one logged player observation that satisfies the adopted novelty rule for a payload/context in a save lineage | runtime/test log records the novelty key and visible delivery | an authored asset; one AEP may produce several NE records |

Relationships:

- one AEP normally references multiple USPs across reaction surfaces;
- a USP may be reused under an allowed context/cooldown, but reuse is not automatically novel;
- one presented AEP may generate zero, one, or multiple NE records depending on what the player actually saw and the adopted novelty key;
- raw display count includes repeats and systemic recombinations and is a fourth, separate metric.

The package must never subtract an NE count from a USP or legacy “authored unit” count. Therefore the previous `1,200 - 712 = 488 reserve` claim is **withdrawn**. It is valid arithmetic over two numbers but has no established unit equivalence.

## 5. Content taxonomy

| Type | Job | Production rule |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor Event Package | make a first, BP, SP, Prestige, ending, or major relationship event causally memorable | complete AEP; bespoke review; multiple surface payloads tracked separately |
| Systemic Flavor | explain a current system/world pressure through variable conditions | layer-specific causal rule; contradiction and repetition tests |
| Short Fragment | maintain social and activity texture | stable USP; cooldown, occurrence cap, and context tags |
| Fictional-person reaction | reveal agency, continuity, interests, boundaries, absence, and return | no power ranking or “good listener” template |
| Sakiya reaction slot | preserve human scale and activity motivation | OWNER ORIGINAL/ACCEPTED only; otherwise unfilled OWNER LINE SLOT |
| World response | show investigation, policy, culture, science, logistics, resistance, or normalization | identify one mechanism, actor, cause, and consequence |
| Activity event | make the activity’s accepted verb and result visible | cannot be reused by changing only the activity noun |
| Archive record | preserve what happened across Prestige and Semantic Retirement | factual, searchable, privacy-safe, and unit-aware |
| Help / recovery | teach or restore a player-visible rule/state | exact, state-specific, and testable; never flavor over missing facts |

## 6. Layer-specific problem engines

| Layer | Primary pressure | Content must show |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | awkward quiet, first contact, small mistakes | individual names and tiny changes matter |
| L2 | continuity and community logistics | people coordinate, disagree, miss context, and create norms |
| L3 | works and networks outlive sessions | catalogs, recommendations, archives, and cross-activity context propagate or misclassify |
| L4 | culture and institutions adapt | procedures, standards, labor, media, and organizations acquire constraints |
| L5 | planetary attention becomes coordination | time, locality, measurement, access, and saturation fail in new ways |
| L6 | observation and language become insufficient | science/civilization build descriptions that become ordinary and fail again |

## 8. Reaction-surface contract

Every AEP declares ordered reaction surfaces:

1. Sakiya OWNER LINE SLOT or an explicit no-line direction.
2. one or more fictional named/cohort people where applicable.
3. room or activity object.
4. world/system response.
5. archive record.
6. optional cross-activity consequence.

Not every package uses all six. A BP candidate uses at least four applicable surfaces. An SP candidate uses all six or records why a surface is intentionally absent. Surface count does not equal Novel Exposure count.

## 12. Repetition and fatigue gates

The binding proof subjects are repetition, missing/unsafe text, layer/activity/tone persistence, route robustness, privacy, and Owner-line status. Durations, route list, case decomposition, selector statistics, tools, and capture method are replaceable Forge choices; the examples below do not mandate a 60-minute/ten-hour protocol.

- no Short Fragment repeats before its configured cooldown;
- no high-frequency context selects the same syntax skeleton three times in one visible sequence;
- a repeated AEP is never presented as a first event;
- sufficiently sustained and varied play has no missing text, raw key, generic filler, or context collision;
- long-horizon evidence preserves distinct layer problem engines;
- coverage addresses normal, zero-gift, mostly silent, video-light, long-offline, recovery, accessibility, and post-goal risks; Forge may combine or replace routes while preserving those proof subjects;
- real-listener identifiers and privacy-sensitive names are zero in default content;
- every official Sakiya line has Owner evidence.

Exact statistical thresholds beyond these gates remain Forge proposals until measured and creatively reviewed.
